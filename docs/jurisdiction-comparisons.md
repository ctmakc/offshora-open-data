# Jurisdiction comparisons

Ten tables that put two jurisdictions side by side, because that is how the choice usually ends: two candidates and a list of questions from the bank, the investor and the tax adviser.

- Repository: [github.com/ctmakc/offshore-jurisdiction-comparisons](https://github.com/ctmakc/offshore-jurisdiction-comparisons)
- Readable version: [ctmakc.github.io/offshore-jurisdiction-comparisons](https://ctmakc.github.io/offshore-jurisdiction-comparisons/)
- Source pages: [all comparisons on offshora.com](https://offshora.com/en/comparisons/)

## Rows you will find in most tables

Governing law, registry, who files documents (usually a licensed registered agent), how beneficial ownership is recorded, the yearly obligations, tax on profits and the status on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions of 9 October 2026.

Two examples of what the rows say: since 2 January 2025 a BVI company files beneficial ownership information with the registry through the VIRRGIN system, and a Delaware LLC pays a $400 tax by 1 June without filing an annual report, while a Wyoming company is dissolved after 60 days without its annual report.

Each table ends with short "choose this if" scenarios that link to the full comparison page.
