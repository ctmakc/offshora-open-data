# Annual compliance calendar

What a company owes its registry every year after incorporation, and what happens when a deadline is missed. Eight jurisdictions, one row each, with a link to the registry and to the Offshora country guide.

- Repository: [github.com/ctmakc/offshore-company-compliance-calendar](https://github.com/ctmakc/offshore-company-compliance-calendar)
- Guide: [annual maintenance of an offshore company](https://offshora.com/en/guides/annual-offshore-maintenance/)

## Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `id` | Short key: `bvi`, `panama`, `nevis`, `uk`, `hong-kong`, `singapore`, `delaware`, `wyoming` |
| `jurisdiction` | Name of the jurisdiction (the CSV uses Russian names) |
| `registry`, `registry_url` | The authority that receives filings and fees |
| `annual_obligations` | What is due each year and when |
| `late_filing` | Penalty or consequence of a missed deadline |
| `guide` | Offshora page with the full explanation |

## Selected rows

| Jurisdiction | Due every year | If late |
|---|---|---|
| BVI | Registry fee; financial return to the agent within 9 months of year end | 10% surcharge in the first two months, 50% later |
| Nevis | Renewal fee USD 300 (IBC and LLC) | USD 200 in the first six months, USD 400 after |
| Hong Kong | NAR1 within 42 days of the anniversary, HK$105 | Higher fee of HK$870 to HK$3,480 |
| Wyoming | Annual report and license tax from $60 | Administrative dissolution after 60 days |
