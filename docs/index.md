# Offshora Open Data

This site documents four small open datasets about running a company abroad. They answer the questions that come up after a founder has picked a business model and before the company is registered: which of two jurisdictions fits, which documents a bank will ask for, how the tax arithmetic changes by business type, and what has to be filed every year once the company exists.

All four datasets were compiled from the free knowledge base of [Offshora](https://offshora.com/), a company formation and corporate services provider working since 2016 with more than 20 jurisdictions. Every row keeps the URL of the page it came from, and those pages cite the laws and registries behind each figure.

## A note on accuracy

Rates, registry fees and deadlines change, sometimes more than once a year. The datasets carry an `as_of` date; treat anything older than a few months as a starting point and confirm it with the registry or tax authority before you act. Nothing here is tax or legal advice for a specific case.
