# Corporate KYC and KYB checklist

Most delays in opening an account for an international company come from a document package that is incomplete or tells two different stories. This dataset turns the onboarding questions of banks, electronic money institutions (EMIs) and payment providers into lists that can be ticked off before applying.

- Repository: [github.com/ctmakc/corporate-kyc-kyb-checklist](https://github.com/ctmakc/corporate-kyc-kyb-checklist)
- Readable version: [ctmakc.github.io/corporate-kyc-kyb-checklist](https://ctmakc.github.io/corporate-kyc-kyb-checklist/)
- Main source page: [Corporate KYC documents](https://offshora.com/en/guides/corporate-kyc-documents/)

## Blocks

1. **What the international standard requires.** The four customer due diligence measures of FATF Recommendation 10: identify the customer, identify and verify the beneficial owner, understand the purpose of the relationship, monitor it.
2. **The company's document package.** Corporate documents, the ownership chart down to each individual, documents on people, the business, the money and CRS tax self-certification.
3. **Who can see beneficial ownership data.** Thresholds and access in the EU, the UK PSC register, Hong Kong, the BVI and the US.
4. **Seven steps of a bank review** and **a package worth preparing in advance**, from the [foreign company bank account guide](https://offshora.com/en/guides/foreign-company-bank-account/).
5. **EMI vs bank.** Deposits, safeguarding versus deposit guarantee (EUR 100,000 in the EU, GBP 120,000 in the UK), interest, credit and what happens in insolvency, from [EMI vs bank account](https://offshora.com/en/guides/emi-vs-bank-account/).
6. **Payment processing checks** before registering the company, and **trading company documents** for goods that cross borders.
7. **Common mistakes**, such as giving the bank, the EMI and the agent different versions of the structure.

## Using it as a checklist

The `items` arrays are plain sentences, so they can be rendered as a checklist in any tool. Keep one person responsible for answering the bank, and make sure the signatory named in the corporate resolution is the same person who goes through the review.
