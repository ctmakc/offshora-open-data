# Sources and corrections

Every figure comes from a published page of the Offshora knowledge base, which exists in English (`/en/`), Ukrainian (`/uk/`) and Russian (`/ru/`). The pages name the statute or registry behind each number, for example the BVI Business Companies Act, the Belize Companies Act 2022, Companies House, ACRA, the Delaware Division of Corporations or Cabinet Decision No. 116 of 2022 in the UAE.

Useful entry points on the site:

- [Offshore jurisdictions overview](https://offshora.com/en/offshornye-zony.html)
- [Services](https://offshora.com/uslugi.html) and [ready-made companies](https://offshora.com/uslugi/gotovye-offshornye-kompanii.html)
- [Contacts](https://offshora.com/kontakty.html)

## Reporting an error

Open an issue in the repository of the dataset concerned and quote the row `id` and the source URL. Corrections are applied to the source page first and then to the data.
