# Company structure tax tables

The right company form depends on the business model first: where the team sits, who pays and how money moves. These seventeen tables and lists start from the business type and then add the jurisdiction facts that change the arithmetic.

- Repository: [github.com/ctmakc/company-structure-tax-tables](https://github.com/ctmakc/company-structure-tax-tables)
- Readable version: [ctmakc.github.io/company-structure-tax-tables](https://ctmakc.github.io/company-structure-tax-tables/)
- Example source: [International company for an IT agency](https://offshora.com/en/industries/it-agency-international-company/)

## By business type

| id | Business | Compared options |
|---|---|---|
| `it-agency` | IT agency | UK Ltd, US LLC, Estonian OÜ, a local company |
| `saas` | SaaS product | UK Ltd, Delaware C-Corp or LLC, Estonian OÜ, Singapore |
| `consultants-freelancers` | Freelancer or consultant | UK Ltd, single-member US LLC, Estonian OÜ |
| `crypto-web3` | Crypto and Web3 | Singapore, Dubai (VARA), BVI |
| `holding-company` | International holding | BVI, Hong Kong, an EU holding |

## Jurisdiction facts

- `cyprus-2026-reform`: corporate tax 12.5% to 15%, Special Defence Contribution on actual dividends 17% to 5%, deemed distribution and stamp duty abolished, losses carried forward for 7 years.
- `estonia-ou-profit-tax`: 22/78 on distributed profit; the 14/86 rate ended on 1 January 2025.
- `uae-corporate-tax` and `uae-qfzp-conditions`: 0% up to AED 375,000 and 9% above; a Qualifying Free Zone Person pays 0% on qualifying income if it meets the substance and transfer pricing conditions.
- `georgia-profit-tax` and `georgia-small-business`: tax on distributed profit; the 1% status applies only to individual entrepreneurs up to GEL 500,000 a year.
- `bahamas-ibc`, `marshall-islands-ibc`, `delaware-vs-wyoming-llc-fees`, `setup-uk-bvi-singapore` (24-month cost items) and `cfc-obligations-russia` (notification deadlines and penalties).
